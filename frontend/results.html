Result Publication

Standard operation:

Official results are published

The official examination results are published on the official NAT-TEST website. Please visit the official website for result confirmation.

Score Report PDF

Score Reports are issued in PDF format by the headquarter. We (Khulna Center) will send the Score Report PDF files directly to the examinees by email.

Hard copy Certificates

Certificates will be sent to Khulna Center from the NAT-TEST headquarters as original hard copies. We will distribute the certificates to examinees by postal mail (the postage to be paid by the examinee). In person collection from the site office is allowed for free of charge.

Certificate Collection Rules

schedule

6-Month Preservation

The certificate will be preserved at Khulna Test Center for 6 months from the test date. Examinees must collect the certificates and score-sheet within this 6-month duration, and after this duration, the documents will be disposed.

badge

Required Documents

The certificate will be handed over only to the examinees. They must bring their admit card along with a valid proof of identity, such as an NID or passport.

verified_user

Authorization

In emergency situations, the certificate may be issued to a parent upon presentation of their ID card and a valid authorization letter from the examinee.

grade Performance Grading System (A–E)

The NAT-TEST score report evaluates performance in each question category using a letter-grade system based on the percentage of correct answers.

Grade Correct Answer Rate
A 80.0% or higher
B 60.0% – 79.9%
C 40.0% – 59.9%
D 20.0% – 39.9%
E Less than 20.0%

This grading system helps examinees identify their strengths and weaknesses for future preparation.
