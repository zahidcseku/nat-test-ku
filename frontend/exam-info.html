About the Japanese Language NAT-TEST

The Japanese Language NAT-TEST is an internationally recognized examination designed to measure the Japanese language ability of non-native speakers. Whether you are planning to study in Japan, apply for a work visa, or simply gauge your language progress, the NAT-TEST offers a reliable and high-quality assessment.

Exam Structure & Levels

The NAT-TEST is divided into five levels of difficulty, ranging from the most basic (Level 5) to the most advanced (Level 1).

Level Difficulty Corresponds to Sections Included
Level 1/1Q Advanced JLPT N1 Language Knowledge (Reading/Vocab/Grammar), Reading & Listening
Level 2/2Q Upper-Intermediate JLPT N2 Language Knowledge (Reading/Vocab/Grammar), Reading & Listening
Level 3/3Q Intermediate JLPT N3 Kanji/Vocabulary, Grammar/Reading & Listening
Level 4/4Q Elementary JLPT N4 Kanji/Vocabulary, Grammar/Reading & Listening
Level 5/5Q Basic JLPT N5 Kanji/Vocabulary, Grammar/Reading & Listening

Each level measures general ability across three core categories: Grammar/Vocabulary, Listening, and Reading Comprehension.

Registration Process

To register for the NAT-TEST at our center, please follow the steps below:

1

Online/Offline Application

Visit our registration portal and select your desired exam level. Ensure all personal information matches your valid photo ID.

2

Payment

Complete the registration fee payment via our secure gateway. Please note that registration is only confirmed once the fee is received.

3

Application Ticket

Approximately 1–2 weeks before the exam, you will receive your application ticket via email/mail. This ticket contains your examinee number and test venue details.

4

Exam Day

Arrive at the center with your application ticket and a valid photo ID (Passport or National ID).

info

Note: Levels 1, 3, and 5 usually take place in the morning, while Levels 2 and 4 take place in the afternoon.

Passing Criteria

To pass the NAT-TEST, candidates must meet two requirements:

check_circle

Sectional Minimum

You must score more than 25% in each individual section of the exam.

check_circle

Overall Score

Your total score must exceed the passing threshold for that specific exam (typically around 60%).

emoji_events Certificate of Proficiency

Successful candidates will receive a Certificate of Proficiency and a detailed score report, which serves as official proof of your Japanese language skills for academic and professional purposes.

Scoring Details

Each level has specific scoring sections with maximum scores and minimum passing marks. Candidates must meet both sectional and overall requirements to pass.

Level Scoring Section Maximum Score Sectional Pass Mark Overall Pass Mark
Level 1/1Q Language Knowledge/Reading/Listening 60 19 100
Level 2/2Q Language Knowledge/Reading/Listening 60 19 90
Level 3/3Q Language Knowledge/Reading/Listening 60 19 95
Level 4/4Q Language Knowledge + Reading 120 38 90

info

Note: Levels 1-3 have separate scoring sections for Language Knowledge and Reading, while Levels 4-5 combine these into a single section. All sectional pass marks are approximately 32% of the maximum score for that section.
