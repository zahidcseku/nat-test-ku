Offline Registration Process

Follow these steps to complete your registration offline:

1

Download and Fill the Application Form

Download the offline application form and fill it out completely with accurate information.

download Download Offline Form (PDF)
2

Make the Payment

Pay the application fee of BDT 4,000.00 to the following bank account:

Account Name: Test Site Director

Account Number: 0200025673722

Bank: Agrani Bank Plc.

Branch: Khulna University

Important Instructions:

  • Pay the exact fee amount
  • Do not use online transfer or third-party transfers
  • Keep your payment slip until the exam is over
  • Deposited money cannot be refunded
3

Prepare Required Documents

Gather the following documents:

  • Two passport-size photographs (recent, plain background)
  • Copy of ID card (Passport or National ID)
  • Payment receipt/deposit slip
  • Completed application form
4

Send Documents via Email

Send all documents as attachments to:

offline_registration@nat-test.ku.ac.bd

Include your full name and ID number in the email subject line

5

Receive Confirmation Email

You will receive a confirmation email within 24-48 hours after we receive your application.

6

Receive Admission Ticket

After your application is reviewed and approved, you will receive your admission ticket via email.

Questions? Contact us at info@nat-test.ku.ac.bd
